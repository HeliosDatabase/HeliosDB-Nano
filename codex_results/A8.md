# A8 - Connection recovery after constraint errors

## Verdict

REAL BUG, fixed in the extended-query path. The simple-query and acceptor-leak
surfaces were already safe, but real PG drivers using the extended protocol could
lose or wedge the connection after an Execute-time constraint error.

Final commit: `5b29224 fix(A8): recover connection after extended-query constraint error`.

## Baseline

Checklist symptom: after many constraint errors the daemon appeared to stop
accepting connections. Cross-agent analysis split that into two surfaces:

- Per-connection acceptor permits were already RAII-released on connection close,
  so the semaphore-leak theory was already fixed.
- The extended-query protocol was still wrong: on a constraint error during
  Execute, the handler sent `ReadyForQuery` before the client's `Sync`. Drivers
  such as `tokio_postgres`, asyncpg, and psycopg expect `ErrorResponse`, discard
  until `Sync`, then `ReadyForQuery`; the early RFQ could wedge or close the
  connection.

## Fix

`src/protocol/postgres/handler.rs` now tracks
`awaiting_sync_after_error` for extended-protocol errors.

- Parse/Bind/Execute/Describe/Close errors send `ErrorResponse` and flush without
  early `ReadyForQuery`.
- Frontend messages are discarded until the client's `Sync`.
- `ReadyForQuery` is sent only after that `Sync`.
- The simple-query path remains unchanged.

## Tests

Regression file: `tests/v334_a8_connection_resilience.rs`.

Coverage:

- `a8_connection_accepts_next_query_after_constraint_error`: 150 duplicate-key
  errors on one long-lived simple-query connection, each followed by a working
  `SELECT COUNT(*)`.
- `a8_daemon_keeps_accepting_after_many_constraint_errors`: 25 short-lived
  connections with `max_connections=5`, proving acceptor permits are released.
- `a8_extended_protocol_recovers_after_constraint_error`: duplicate-key error
  through `tokio_postgres::execute`, followed by successful simple and extended
  queries on the same connection.

Final verification recorded during the item loop:

- Later compatibility runs after A14 also kept this regression green.
