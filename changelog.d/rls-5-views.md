**Policies hold through views and materialized views.**

Views: a view is read with the reader's rights, so the policies of the tables under it apply to whoever reads the view, also when the view does not select the column a policy tests. `CREATE VIEW … WITH (security_invoker = true)` names this behaviour and is accepted; it is the default. `WITH (security_invoker = false)` (the view owner's rights) is refused with 0A000; before, the option was accepted and ignored.

Materialized views: before, the stored rows of a materialized view were returned to every reader, whatever the policies of its base tables. Now the base tables' policies are applied to the stored rows when that gives the same result as filtering the base rows first: every column the policy reads reaches the view's output under its own name and unchanged, it is a grouping key wherever the view aggregates, and the view does not mix or drop rows across policy values (`LIMIT`, `DISTINCT ON`, an aggregate with no `GROUP BY`, a window not partitioned by the column, `UNION` without `ALL`, `INTERSECT`, `EXCEPT`, a `UNION ALL` branch that does not read the table, an outer join that can leave the table's side empty, the table read twice, a subquery or a common table expression over the table). When that cannot be shown, reading the view as a role that is subject to a policy fails with 42501, naming the view, the table, the policy and the column or construct. It is never an empty result and never the unfiltered rows. Exempt roles, and the owner of the base table, read the view as before. A materialized view takes no policy of its own.
